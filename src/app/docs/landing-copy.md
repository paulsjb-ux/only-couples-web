## How it works
**Title:** From empty room to private album  
**Body:** Built for couples who want something personal — a bit of fun through to scenes you’d never ask a photographer for.

1. **Lock your faces** — Each of you adds a few reference photos. They stay in your studio. We don’t train on them.  
2. **Start soft** — The first scene is gentle — bed, morning light, the two of you. No shock, no homepage explicit.  
3. **Preview, then decide** — Every image is a preview until you Keep it. Discard removes it. Keep puts it in your private album.  
4. **Invite when you’re ready** — Open the studio alone, or invite your partner. Shared library. Optional hide-from-partner for surprises.

## Privacy
**Title:** This is not a public gallery  
**Body:** The product is the closed album. If it ever feels like a feed, we’ve failed.

- Photos stay in your studio — not used to train a public model  
- No public gallery by default. No SEO of your scenes  
- Delete account and we wipe storage  
- Preview → keep or discard. Nothing auto-saves into the album  
- Hide from partner when you want a surprise  
- Adult-only access, real enough for the way we process payments  

## Who it’s for
**Title:** Built for couples — not for everyone  

1. **Couples already sharing** — You send nudes or play with AI casually. You want it to look like both of you.  
2. **Someone buying for two** — A gift of a private studio — soft enough she’ll open it, deep enough you’ll both stay.  
3. **One consistent “us”** — Not a new stranger every prompt. The same two faces, a relationship-shaped scene list.
